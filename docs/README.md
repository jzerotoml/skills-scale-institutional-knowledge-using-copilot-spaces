# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This collection describes how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, releases, and improves projects. The process is customer-first and iterative, with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and regular opportunities to inspect progress and adapt.

Projects begin with a one-pager, stakeholder alignment, success metrics, risks, and a high-level timeline. Approved work is broken into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and a Definition of Done. During execution, teams track work on a project board, communicate through standups and regular delivery or stakeholder updates, and escalate blockers and risks through defined channels.

Quality is built into the lifecycle through unit, integration, and end-to-end testing, manual QA where needed, automated CI checks, linting, and security scanning. Releases require passing acceptance criteria, smoke testing, rollback planning, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents turn lessons learned into owned and measurable improvement actions.
