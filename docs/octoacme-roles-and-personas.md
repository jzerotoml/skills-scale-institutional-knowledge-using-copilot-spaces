# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering/Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering/Technical Leads provide technical direction and help the delivery team make sound architecture and implementation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical direction, architecture constraints, and engineering standards
- Assess feasibility, effort, dependencies, and technical risks
- Facilitate technical decisions and document significant trade-offs
- Support Developers with design guidance, reviews, and escalation of technical blockers
- Confirm engineering readiness for testing and release

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to evaluate scope, feasibility, and technical trade-offs
- Partner with Project Managers to surface technical risks, dependencies, and delivery impacts
- Guide Developers during implementation and collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and release readiness
- Provide technical context to Stakeholders when decisions require broader alignment

---

## UX/Product Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/Product Designers and Researchers represent user needs and validate that proposed solutions are understandable, accessible, and useful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct discovery and research to understand user needs and workflows
- Create and validate user flows, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Define usability, accessibility, and design acceptance criteria
- Share research findings and design decisions with the delivery team
- Identify usability risks and recommend improvements throughout delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect user needs, product outcomes, and priorities
- Work with Developers and Engineering/Technical Leads to align designs with technical feasibility
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility in validation
- Gather feedback from Stakeholders and help Project Managers coordinate user-focused reviews

---

## Delivery/Release Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery/Release Managers coordinate release readiness, deployment activities, operational dependencies, and launch communications.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release readiness criteria, timelines, and deployment sequencing
- Coordinate environment, migration, operational, and rollback dependencies
- Confirm that release notes, runbooks, support preparation, and smoke tests are ready
- Facilitate go/no-go decisions and track post-release verification
- Coordinate release communications and capture follow-up actions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers to align releases with milestones, risks, and stakeholder communication
- Coordinate with Developers, Engineering/Technical Leads, and QA/Testing on technical readiness and verification
- Work with Product Managers to confirm scope, acceptance criteria, and launch outcomes
- Coordinate with Stakeholders and Customer Support/Operations Representatives on launch readiness

---

## Security/Privacy Representatives

### Role Summary
Security/Privacy Representatives identify and manage security, privacy, and compliance needs throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Review risks, proposed designs, data handling, and controls
- Coordinate threat assessments, security testing, and remediation tracking
- Advise on incident escalation and release risk acceptance
- Document required safeguards and evidence for approval or audit

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to translate regulatory and customer expectations into requirements
- Work with Developers and Engineering/Technical Leads on secure design and implementation
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation
- Help Project Managers maintain the risk register and escalate unresolved risks to Stakeholders

---

## Data/Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data/Analytics Leads define how project outcomes will be measured and ensure that instrumentation and reporting support evidence-based decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans, metrics, event instrumentation, and data quality expectations
- Identify baseline values and target outcomes with the delivery team
- Validate telemetry and reporting before and after release
- Analyze results and communicate insights, limitations, and follow-up recommendations
- Maintain documentation for metric definitions and data ownership

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect success metrics to product goals
- Work with Developers and Engineering/Technical Leads to implement reliable telemetry
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to validate instrumentation and data quality
- Provide Project Managers and Stakeholders with outcome reporting for reviews and retrospectives

---

## Customer Support/Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support/Operations Representatives bring frontline customer and operational context to planning, delivery, and release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Identify customer-impacting workflows, operational constraints, and support risks
- Contribute support requirements, knowledge-base content, training needs, and runbooks
- Participate in acceptance and readiness reviews for customer-facing changes
- Prepare support teams for launch and monitor early feedback after release
- Escalate recurring customer or operational issues to the delivery team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to represent customer impact and prioritize feedback
- Work with Project Managers on rollout plans, communications, dependencies, and risk tracking
- Collaborate with Delivery/Release Managers on launch support and post-release monitoring
- Provide Developers, QA/Testing, and Stakeholders with operational scenarios and customer evidence

---

## Executive Sponsors/Business Owners

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors/Business Owners provide strategic sponsorship, make governance decisions, and remove organizational barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment, priority, funding, and success expectations
- Approve major scope, timeline, risk, and resource trade-offs
- Resolve escalated cross-team conflicts and secure organizational support
- Participate in decision gates and review progress against intended outcomes
- Champion adoption and accountability for business results

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Receive status, risk, and decision updates from Project Managers
- Align with Product Managers on product outcomes and prioritization
- Consult Engineering/Technical Leads and other specialists when approving significant trade-offs
- Provide direction to Stakeholders and dependent teams when escalation or sponsorship is needed
